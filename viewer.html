Pipeline Visualizer

Transformations and coordinate space changes can be interpreted as deforming the spatial grid of the world.

If you are not familiar with this way of interpreting matrix products and other transformations, you can check 3Blue1Brown's Linear Transformations Chapter .

In this visualization, you are seing a top-down view of a 3D scene. Each grid represents the X (red) and Z (blue) axes of its respective coordinate space.

Apart from the numbered labels at the end of each line, you can visually identify the positive sides of the grid because they are missing the last line.
